About

About CDC Portal

CDC Portal is a centralized, role-based account management system built for the Career Development Centre. Admins create faculty and student accounts, while each role sees only what it is allowed to see — secured end to end with JWT authentication.

Centralized Accounts

One place for the Admin to provision faculty and student logins, keeping records consistent and easy to manage.

Role-Based Access

Admins, Faculty and Students each get a tailored dashboard. Sensitive actions are locked to the Admin role only.

Secure Authentication

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and sessions use signed JWTs. Student QR codes carry a non-sensitive identifier only.
